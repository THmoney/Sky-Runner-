About Sky Runner

Sky Runner is a free browser-based endless runner, inspired by the classic offline dinosaur game. Jump and dodge your way past colorful obstacles, chase a high score, and use rewarded video ads for extra lives when you're close to a new personal best.

No download or install needed — it runs straight in your browser. Built and maintained independently as a small side project.
